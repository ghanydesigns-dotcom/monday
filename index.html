M.O.N.D.A.Y

Ghany's FRIDAY — a free panel for Adobe Illustrator that does the repetitive work so designers can design.

Tools
  • Create — new documents with the right size, bleed, trim guides and artboards: Digital (social), Print (A-series, business card, custom) and Banner formats.
  • Mosaic — arrange images across artboards in a tidy grid.
  • Moodboard — place many images into a packed board that fits the artboard, with Fit or Fill (clipped) modes and your own margin and gap.
  • Text scripts — insert and edit text in bulk, number sequences, align baselines and replace formatted text, built on open-source scripts by Sergey Osokin (MIT).

Requires Adobe Illustrator 2020 (v24) or later on Windows or macOS.
