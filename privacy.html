Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

M.O.N.D.A.Y ("the plugin") is a free plugin for Adobe Illustrator made by Mohamed Abdelghany. This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it.

What the plugin collects

Nothing. The plugin has no accounts, analytics, tracking, advertising or telemetry, and it does not send your files, images, settings or personal information to the developer or to any third party.

What stays on your computer

Panel preferences are stored locally on your device. Documents you create and images you place stay in your own files and are never transmitted. Uninstalling the plugin removes its data.

AI training

The plugin is not, and will never be, used to train AI models, and the developer does not collect any content that could be used for that.

Links

The Info tab contains links (for example support and payment links). They open in your browser only when you click them; those services have their own privacy policies.

Children

The plugin is intended for users of Adobe Illustrator and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
