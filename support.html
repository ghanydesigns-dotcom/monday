When you write, please include

  • Your Illustrator version (Help → About Illustrator) and operating system.
  • The M.O.N.D.A.Y version (top of the panel).
  • Which tool you used and the message shown in the panel.
  • A screenshot if possible. The Info tab also has a Report a bug button.

Common questions

The panel is missing from Window → Extensions. Restart Illustrator after installing, and check the plugin is listed in Creative Cloud or your ZXP installer.

A text script shows a security prompt. Click Allow; the panel runs the script inside Illustrator.

Is my work uploaded anywhere? No. Everything runs locally on your computer.
